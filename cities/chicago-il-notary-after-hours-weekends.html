What to ask before you leave

  • Are you commissioned and available right now?
  • Do you handle my document type?
  • What is the total fee, including travel or after-hours charges?
  • What ID and witnesses do I need?
  • If online, will the receiving party accept remote notarization?

Hours and availability can change, especially for mobile providers. A business being open does not guarantee a notary is immediately available. Call or text before relying on any listing.

Remote online notarization is not appropriate for every document or transaction. If the receiving organization requires wet ink or an in-person notarization, use a local mobile or walk-in option instead.
